Launch checklist

For you, the designer. “Done” means built into this demo. “Your input” means the shop owner must supply or confirm something. This is a practical checklist, not legal advice: have a lawyer or CA review the policies before launch.

ItemStatusWhat was done or what you must do
Colour contrastDoneEvery text and control colour pair was calculated against WCAG 2.2 AA in both light and dark mode. The lowest text ratio is 5.85:1 (needs 4.5) and the lowest control or border ratio is 4.94:1 (needs 3). Recheck whenever you change colours.
Alt text on imagesDoneEvery image has a description. If you add photos, write what each one shows. Use empty alt text only for purely decorative images.
Refund policyYour inputPage written. Fill the highlighted time limits with what you will really do.
Privacy policyYour inputPage written to match the data this form collects. Fill the highlighted retention period and storage tool.
Fix accessibilityDoneSkip link, headings and landmarks, visible focus, large touch targets, labelled fields, error summary, screen reader announcements, dark mode, reduced motion. Still test with only a keyboard and with a free screen reader (NVDA, or TalkBack on Android) before launch.
Remove fake reviewsDoneNo reviews, star ratings, counts or testimonials. Add real ones later only with the customer’s permission.
Terms and conditionsYour inputPage written. Fill delivery area, charges and court city.
Check third-party embedsDoneNone. No maps, videos, social feeds, chat widgets or web fonts (the site uses fonts already on the visitor’s device). Add an embed only if you also update the privacy and cookie pages.
Check copyright on imagesDoneAll pictures are drawings made for this demo. For a real shop, use your own photos of your own products, never images copied from Google or other bakeries.
Cookies policyDonePage lists exactly what the site stores. Update it if you add any tool.
Check trackingDoneNo analytics, ad pixels or fingerprinting. To verify: open the browser’s developer tools, go to the Network tab, reload, and confirm that no requests go to other websites.
Form consentDoneUnticked checkbox that must be ticked, plain-language purpose, how to withdraw, link to the privacy notice. No pre-ticked boxes and no promotions.
Check local lawsYour inputCheck with a professional: India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025 (the main notice and consent duties apply from mid-May 2027, so building to them now is sensible), the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 (seller details, grievance officer, refund terms), FSSAI licensing and labelling for food, and GST if you are registered.
Clear button labelsDoneButtons say what they do: “Add one Chocolate truffle cake”, “Reject optional cookies”, “Place order”.
Check for cookie consentDoneNotice with equally prominent Reject and Accept buttons, choice remembered, reopened from “Cookie settings”. The site has no optional cookies yet, and any future tool must load only when the choice allows it.
Add real business detailsYour inputContact page has highlighted slots for name, address, phone, email, FSSAI number, GSTIN and grievance officer. The bar at the top counts what is left. Never invent details.
Only collect necessary dataDoneName, mobile, address (delivery only, cleared if you switch to pickup) and an optional note. No email, no date of birth, no location, no payment details.
Keyboard-friendly formsDoneEvery field has a visible label, correct input type and autocomplete, Enter submits, errors are listed and focus moves to the first problem, radios are grouped, nothing traps focus.
Remove unsupported claimsDoneNo “best”, “freshest”, “100% natural” or similar. Only describe what you can prove, and confirm delivery times and allergens before publishing.
